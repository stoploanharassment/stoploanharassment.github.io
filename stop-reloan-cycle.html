Break the Re-loan Cycle

Your financial recovery begins with the decision to stop borrowing out of fear.

Rahul's Story

An illustrative story of a borrower caught in a debt trap.

Rahul took a small payday loan to manage an unexpected expense, believing he would repay it with his next salary. When the repayment date arrived, he was short of money. Afraid of collection calls, embarrassment, and damage to his credit score, he took another loan to repay the first.

One month became three, and three became six. Every month, he borrowed more just to survive. His salary was consumed by repayments, interest, and processing fees. He was no longer borrowing out of necessity but out of fear.

One day, Rahul asked himself,

“If I keep borrowing to repay my loans, when will this ever end?”

He realised that another loan would only provide temporary relief and prolong his financial struggle. He decided to stop repeat borrowing, face his financial situation, and work towards a realistic repayment plan.

Does Rahul's story sound familiar?

Breaking the cycle may be difficult, but continuing it will only deepen the problem. Your financial recovery begins with the decision to stop borrowing out of fear.

Break the cycle. Face the situation. Make a plan. Rebuild your life.

Your debt is a chapter of your life, not your entire story.

This content is for educational and awareness purposes only. Stopping repeat borrowing does not eliminate existing repayment obligations. Seek qualified financial or legal advice where needed.
