Before submitting

  • Do not share Aadhaar/PAN numbers, addresses, account numbers, OTPs, or other people's contact details.
  • Do not upload screenshots, recordings, or financial documents through this story form.
  • Submissions are manually reviewed; publication is not guaranteed.
  • This is an independent awareness initiative, not an emergency or official complaint service.
