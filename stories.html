Before you submit

  • Do not include Aadhaar/PAN numbers, addresses, bank account numbers, OTPs, or other people's contact details.
  • Do not upload recordings, screenshots, or financial documents through the story form.
  • Stories will be reviewed manually. Submission does not guarantee publication.
  • We will seek explicit publication consent, and use a pseudonym if you prefer.
  • This website is an independent awareness initiative, not an emergency or legal complaint channel.
