Protect Your Loved Ones from Harassment

Loan recovery harassment may not always be limited to the borrower. Some recovery agents may attempt to pressure borrowers by repeatedly calling their family members, friends, relatives, and colleagues, even when these individuals have no involvement in the loan.

Protecting your loved ones from unnecessary stress, embarrassment, and harassment is equally important. Inform them about the possibility of unwanted calls and help them secure their phones.

1. Protect Your Family and Friends from Recovery Calls

Consider purchasing Truecaller Premium for your parents, siblings, close friends, or other family members who may receive excessive recovery calls.

  • Enable advanced spam protection.
  • Enable automatic blocking of known spam numbers.
  • Enable blocking of hidden or private numbers.
  • Restrict incoming calls to saved contacts only, where supported.
  • Ask your loved ones not to disclose personal information to recovery agents.
  • Preserve call logs and screenshots as evidence of harassment.

Official website: https://www.truecaller.com/

Call-blocking features may vary depending on the device, operating system, and subscription.

2. Enable Contacts-Only Calling for Family and Friends

If your family members or friends are receiving repeated calls from recovery agents, help them configure their phones to accept calls only from saved contacts.

This can significantly reduce unwanted interruptions and protect your loved ones from unnecessary stress and harassment.

Device/Application Recommended Settings
Android Settings → Do Not Disturb → Calls → Contacts Only
iPhone Settings → Focus → Do Not Disturb → People → Contacts Only
Truecaller Premium Settings → Block → Block numbers not in the phonebook, where supported
WhatsApp Settings → Privacy → Calls → Silence Unknown Callers

Disable the Allow Repeated Calls option in Do Not Disturb settings to prevent unknown callers from bypassing the restriction by calling repeatedly.

Ensure important numbers, such as doctors, emergency contacts, and workplaces, are saved in their phonebooks. These settings may also silence legitimate calls from unknown numbers.

Important Note

These restrictions are specifically recommended for affected family members and friends.

As a borrower, maintain a verified communication channel, preferably email, with your lenders to discuss repayment, settlement, and grievances.

3. Protect Yourself and Your Family from OTP Bombing

OTP bombing is a form of digital harassment where someone repeatedly triggers automated verification messages, registration requests, or calls using another person's mobile number.

Receiving hundreds of unwanted OTP messages can cause panic, disrupt daily activities, and make it difficult to identify genuine security alerts.

Some SMS and call bombing websites claim to provide a number-protection or exclusion feature. Adding your mobile number to such a protection list may prevent that particular platform from targeting it.

Important Privacy Precautions

These are independent third-party websites, not government-approved protection services. Their safety, availability, and effectiveness have not been independently verified.

Submitting your mobile number to an untrusted website may expose you to further privacy risks. Avoid submitting personal information to untrusted services.

Never provide OTPs, passwords, banking information, or other sensitive personal details. Do not submit another person's mobile number without their consent.

Stop Loan Harassment does not endorse these platforms or their activities. These links are provided solely for awareness of their claimed number-exclusion features.

Protection is generally limited to the individual platform. There is no verified universal protection list that guarantees complete prevention of OTP or call bombing.

DND primarily restricts unsolicited commercial communications. It does not guarantee complete protection against OTP bombing or abusive recovery calls.

Preserve screenshots, call logs, phone numbers, dates, and timestamps before raising a complaint. Encourage affected family members and friends to report harassment directly as well.

Remember, Your Family Is Not Responsible for Your Debt

Your financial challenges are yours to resolve, but your family members and friends should not be subjected to harassment, intimidation, or humiliation because of them.

Do not allow the fear of recovery agents contacting your loved ones to force you into taking another loan. Borrowing more money out of fear will only prolong your financial struggles.

Take preventive measures, protect your loved ones, preserve evidence, and report inappropriate recovery practices through the appropriate channels.

Your loved ones deserve peace of mind, and so do you.

You have a repayment responsibility, but you also have a right to dignity, privacy, and peace.
Protect your loved ones. Set your boundaries. Break the cycle of fear.
