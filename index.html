Why we exist

Payday loan awareness

Before taking another short-term loan

Check the total cost

Look beyond the advertised amount. Review disbursal deductions, interest, fees, penalties, and the actual repayment date.

Verify the lender

Identify the actual lending entity and check the loan agreement, Key Facts Statement, and official grievance contact.

Avoid a debt spiral

Taking a new expensive loan to repay an old one can deepen financial distress. Compare safer alternatives before borrowing.

Know your rights

Debt collection should not involve abuse

Rules and complaint routes depend on the lender and circumstances. For RBI-regulated entities and their agents, applicable fair-practice and recovery guidelines prohibit intimidation and harassment.

Respectful communication

Threats, abusive language, and public humiliation are not acceptable recovery practices.

Privacy matters

Unnecessary disclosure of your debt to unrelated third parties may raise serious privacy and conduct concerns.

Ask for written records

Request account statements, charges, official payment instructions, and written confirmation of any agreed repayment arrangement.

Educational information only; not legal advice. Check current official rules and obtain qualified advice for your situation.

Practical next steps

What to do if recovery calls become abusive

Step 1

Preserve evidence

Keep dates, phone numbers, messages, call logs, and lawful recordings. Do not publish other people's private details.

Step 2

Write to the lender

Use the official grievance channel. Describe specific incidents, ask for the conduct to stop, and request a written response.

Step 3

Escalate appropriately

For eligible RBI-regulated entities, review the RBI complaint portal. Eligibility and waiting periods apply.

Step 4

Report threats or cyber abuse

For suspected cybercrime in India, visit cybercrime.gov.in. For immediate danger, contact police.

Never share OTPs, passwords, banking login details, or unredacted identity documents with unknown callers.

Why this project matters
